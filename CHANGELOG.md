## 0.2.0

* Added `SearchyField.elevated` constructor with shadow and elevation support.
* Added `SearchyField.outlined` constructor with customizable border colors.
* Extended `SearchyScaffold` to fully support and forward all standard `Scaffold` properties (such as `floatingActionButton`, `drawer`, `bottomNavigationBar`, `backgroundColor`, `resizeToAvoidBottomInset`, etc.).
* Updated and enhanced example app to showcase all style variants and bar placements.

## 0.1.0

* Initial release of `searchy`.
* Added `SearchyField` with customizable input decorations, clear button, and filled factory variant.
* Added `SearchyBar` with placement modes (`inline`, `action`, `bottom`).
* Added `SearchyScaffold` for search-oriented view swapping and focus handling.
